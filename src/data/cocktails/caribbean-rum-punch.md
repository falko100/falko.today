---
name: Caribbean Rum Punch
category: Punch / Party Drink
IBA: null
glass: Highball glass
isAlcoholic: true
image: /images/cocktails/caribbean-rum-punch.svg
ingredients:
  - name: Dark rum
    measurement: 60 ml
  - name: Orange juice
    measurement: 60 ml
  - name: Pineapple juice
    measurement: 60 ml
  - name: Lime juice
    measurement: 30 ml
  - name: Grenadine
    measurement: 15 ml
  - name: Angostura bitters
    measurement: 2 dashes
---

Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice. Shake well and strain into a highball glass filled with ice. Garnish with a slice of orange and a cherry.
